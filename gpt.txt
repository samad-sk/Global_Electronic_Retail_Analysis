Here's the analysis rewritten simply, with clear actions:

---

**The Big Picture**

Every category grew steadily, hit its best year in 2019, then started falling. By 2021 profits collapsed — but this is likely because 2021 data is only a partial year, not a full collapse.

---

**What the Business Should Do:**

**1. Put most of your money into Computers**
It's your biggest earner by far — nearly 3x bigger than the next category at peak. More stock, more marketing, better supplier deals. This is your main product. Treat it like one.

**2. Investigate Home Appliances urgently**
It was your #1 category in 2016 but started losing ground before everyone else did. Something went wrong there specifically — maybe pricing, maybe competition, maybe product range. Find out why before investing more into it.

**3. Make a decision on Games & Toys**
It never made real money across 6 years. Either find out if there's a specific reason to keep it (loyal niche customers, low cost to maintain) — or cut it and redirect that energy elsewhere.

**4. Don't panic about 2021**
The 2021 numbers look like a disaster but are almost certainly incomplete data. Before making any major cuts or decisions, confirm whether 2021 is a full year or just a few months of data.

**5. Understand what drove 2019's peak**
Was it a specific product launch? A marketing push? Economic conditions? If you know what caused the peak, you know what to try and repeat.

---

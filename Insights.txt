Scatter chart of profit margin 

In your scatter chart:

* **X-axis = Sales**
* **Y-axis = Profit Margin %**
* **Bubble size = Profit**

So position means this:

---

### 1. Top-right (High Sales + High Margin)

* Big and efficient business
* Best scenario
* Strong products/categories

---

### 2. Top-left (Low Sales + High Margin)

* Small but efficient
* Niche products
* Good opportunity to scale

---

### 3. Bottom-right (High Sales + Low Margin)

* Sells a lot but earns less per sale
* Risk area
* Needs pricing or cost fix

---

### 4. Bottom-left (Low Sales + Low Margin)

* Weak area
* Low demand + low efficiency
* Usually not important for growth

---

### Bubble size meaning (important)

* Big bubble = high total profit
* Small bubble = low total profit

So a point can be:

* Top-right but small → efficient but low scale
* Bottom-right but big → high sales still making money

---

### Simple way to read it

```text id="c1m8qz"
Right = sells more
Up = earns better per sale
Big = earns more total money
```
That’s all the bubble chart is showing.
